## 1. Overview
Welcome to **CineFlix** ("we", "our", or "us"), developed by **Saad Afridi**. CineFlix is a movie and television discovery and information application designed to help cinema enthusiasts discover trending movies, explore TV shows, view official trailers, check ratings, and browse cast details.

We respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding information collection, usage, and disclosure when you use our mobile application.

---

## 2. Information Collection and Use

### A. Personal Information
**CineFlix does not require user registration or account creation.** We do not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical address, or phone number.

### B. Local Device Storage
Any data you generate within the app—such as:
- Saved **Favorites / Watchlist**
- Recent search queries
- App preferences (e.g., dark/light theme, mute preference)

is stored **exclusively on your local device** using local on-device storage. This data is never uploaded to any remote server and can be cleared at any time by clearing the application data in your device settings or uninstalling the app.

---

## 3. Third-Party Services and APIs

CineFlix integrates third-party application programming interfaces (APIs) and services to retrieve movie metadata, ratings, and video streams:

### A. The Movie Database (TMDB) API
- CineFlix uses the TMDB API to provide movie and TV show data, posters, backdrops, cast information, and watch provider links.
- **Attribution:** *This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.*
- For information on how TMDB handles data, please review the [TMDB Terms of Use](https://www.themoviedb.org/documentation/api/terms-of-use).

### B. YouTube API Services
- CineFlix provides official promotional trailers, teasers, and featurettes via embedded YouTube player technology (`youtube_player_flutter`).
- CineFlix **never** downloads, extracts, scrapes, or stores raw video binaries. All video playback occurs through the official YouTube player framework.
- By using video playback features in CineFlix, users agree to be bound by the [YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

### C. OMDb API
- CineFlix optionally queries the Open Movie Database (OMDb) API to display IMDb ratings, Metascore, and Rotten Tomatoes score badges. No user data is sent during these requests.

---

## 4. Device Permissions

CineFlix requests only the minimum Android permissions strictly necessary for its operation:

- `android.permission.INTERNET`: Required to communicate with TMDB, OMDb, and stream promotional video trailers via YouTube.
- `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`: Required to detect internet connectivity and notify users when offline.

We **do not** request permissions for camera, microphone, contacts, location, SMS, or external storage read/write.

---

## 5. Non-Hosting & Content Disclaimer

CineFlix is strictly an informational and discovery application:
- CineFlix **does not host, store, stream, or distribute full-length copyrighted movies or television episodes**.
- All media assets, trademarks, posters, and summaries are the intellectual property of their respective copyright holders and studios.
- Video clips are limited to promotional trailers and teasers hosted and authorized on YouTube by copyright owners and studios.

## 6. Children's Privacy

CineFlix does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under the age of 13. Since the app does not collect personal data from any user, it is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and GDPR-K.

## 7. Security

We take all reasonable precautions to maintain the security of our application. All network communications with TMDB, OMDb, and YouTube are encrypted via standard Secure Socket Layer / Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS).
