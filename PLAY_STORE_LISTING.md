## 3. Full Description (Up to 4000 characters)

Looking for your next favorite movie or binge-worthy TV series? **CineFlix** is your ultimate cinema companion! Explore thousands of trending films, top-rated TV series, official HD trailers, full cast bios, user ratings, and legal streaming availability — all in one sleek, modern interface.

Powered by **The Movie Database (TMDB)** and seamless YouTube trailer streaming, CineFlix puts the entire universe of entertainment at your fingertips.

---

### 🌟 KEY FEATURES

🎬 **Discover Movies & TV Shows**
- Browse what's Trending Today, Popular, Top Rated, and Upcoming.
- Seamlessly toggle between Movies and TV Series with a single tap.
- Filter by genres: Action, Comedy, Sci-Fi, Drama, Horror, Romance, Animation, and more.

▶️ **Seamless HD Video Trailer Playback**
- Watch official trailers, teasers, behind-the-scenes clips, and featurettes directly in the app.
- Interactive inline video player with backdrop preview fallback.
- True full-screen mode with automatic landscape rotation and responsive playback controls.
- Categorized video tabs: All, Trailers, Teasers, Clips, and Behind The Scenes.
- Playlist queue with automatic sequential playback of related trailers.

⭐ **Comprehensive Ratings & Critic Scores**
- View community ratings and vote counts powered by TMDB.
- Integrated IMDb scores and Rotten Tomatoes critic percentages via OMDb.

📺 **Where to Watch (Legal Streaming Availability)**
- Find out where to legally stream, rent, or buy any title in your country (Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, Apple TV+, and more).

🎭 **Cast & Crew Insights**
- Explore full actor profiles, character roles, and director filmographies.
- High-resolution production stills and movie poster galleries with pinch-to-zoom support.

🔍 **Smart Instant Search**
- Fast real-time search across thousands of movies and TV titles with instant poster previews and release dates.

❤️ **Personal Watchlist & Favorites**
- Save your must-watch movies and TV shows locally with a single tap.
- 100% offline access to your saved favorites list.

🌙 **Premium Cinematic UI**
- Deep dark mode aesthetics with smooth fluid animations and responsive glassmorphism touches.
- Built with Google's modern Flutter framework for silky-smooth 60fps/120fps performance.
