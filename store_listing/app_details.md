## 3. Full Description

```text
Welcome to CineFlix — your ultimate cinematic guide for discovering movies, watching official trailers, checking ratings, and keeping track of your favorites!

Whether you are looking for what's playing in theatres right now, upcoming blockbuster premieres, top-rated classics, or trending sensations, CineFlix puts the entire cinematic universe in the palm of your hand.

KEY FEATURES:

✨ DISCOVER & BROWSE
• Explore curated rows: Now Playing, Popular, Top Rated, and Upcoming.
• Interactive backdrop carousel showcasing high-resolution movie banners.
• Browse complete details: plot synopsis, release date, runtime, genres, budget, revenue, and production companies.

🎬 WATCH OFFICIAL TRAILERS
• Instant in-app trailer playback powered by YouTube.
• Seamless fullscreen mode with automatic orientation adaptation.
• Playlist selector to switch between teasers, trailers, behind-the-scenes featurettes, and clips.

⭐ RATINGS & VERIFIED REVIEWS
• Dual rating integration: TMDB community scores and OMDb (IMDb / Rotten Tomatoes / Metacritic) badges.
• Real-time critic insights and audience feedback.

🔍 POWERFUL SEARCH & FILTER
• Instant live search as you type across thousands of titles.
• Filter by movie genre or sort by popularity and release dates.
• Clean, responsive grid layout for quick exploration.

❤️ PERSONAL FAVORITES
• Save movies to your offline-accessible Favorites list with a single tap.
• Quick access from the dedicated Favorites tab.
• Instant synchronization across your local session.

📺 WHERE TO WATCH
• Discover available streaming providers, rental platforms, and digital purchase options powered by JustWatch via TMDB.

🎨 MODERN, ELEGANT DESIGN
• Crafted with Flutter & Material 3 guidelines.
• Premium dark theme default with an elegant light theme option.
• Smooth micro-interactions, responsive typography, and tactile animations.

🔒 PRIVACY & TRANSPARENCY
• No account registration or login required.
• No invasive tracking, third-party analytics, or background data harvesting.
• Clear copyright and attribution details accessible directly in Settings.

---

DISCLAIMER & LEGAL ATTRIBUTION:
• CineFlix does NOT stream, host, download, or provide full copyrighted movies or TV shows.
• CineFlix uses the TMDB API for metadata and promotional media but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
• Movie ratings and additional metadata are provided via OMDb API.
• All trailers are streamed via the official YouTube embedded player in compliance with YouTube API Terms of Service.
• All trademarks, movie titles, posters, and imagery belong to their respective copyright owners.
```
