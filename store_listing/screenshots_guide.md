# CineFlix — Store Screenshots & Visual Assets Guide

This guide details the 6 recommended marketing screenshots for Google Play Store and Apple App Store listings, including exact visual composition and promotional banner copy.

---

## Technical Specifications

### Google Play Store
- **Format:** JPEG or 24-bit PNG (no alpha)
- **Minimum dimension:** 320px; **Maximum dimension:** 3840px
- **Recommended Phone Aspect Ratio:** 16:9 or 9:16 (e.g., 1080 x 2400 px or 1080 x 1920 px)
- **Minimum quantity:** 4 screenshots; **Recommended:** 6 to 8

### Apple App Store
- **iPhone 6.7" Display (iPhone 16 Pro Max / 15 Pro Max / 14 Pro Max):** 1290 x 2796 px (Portrait)
- **iPhone 6.5" Display (iPhone 11 Pro Max / XS Max):** 1242 x 2688 px (Portrait)
- **iPad Pro 12.9" Display (6th Gen):** 2048 x 2732 px (Portrait)

## Screenshot Caption Overlay Template Styling
- **Background Tone:** Deep Charcoal (`#0B0E14`) or Midnight Gradient (`#0B0E14` -> `#1A1D26`)
- **Headline Font:** Poppins Bold, White (`#FFFFFF`), 48pt
- **Subtitle Font:** Poppins Medium, Cyan/Teal Accent (`#00B4D8`) or Coral Red (`#E50914`), 24pt
- **Device Mockup:** Minimalist flat bezel (Pixel 7 / iPhone 15 Pro) with gentle shadow drop.
